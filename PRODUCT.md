# PRODUCT.md — RanMet

## Product Vision
RanMet is an AI-powered social connection platform that blends intelligent matchmaking, short-form video entertainment (RanVideo), real-time community boards (RanNews), and spatial audio/voice lounges (RanWorld).

## Target Audience
Creators, developers, gamers, and youth communities globally who want authentic connections based on hobbies, vibe, and real-time interactions without toxic ads or spam.

## Core Pillars
1. **AI Matching Radar:** Real-time concentric radar scan calculating multi-dimensional compatibility and auto-generating icebreakers.
2. **RanVideo Short-Form:** Full-width desktop cinema theater split feed and edge-to-edge mobile player with AI Vision multimodal moderation.
3. **RanNews Feed:** 3-column modern community timeline with instant 1-tap multilingual AI translation.
4. **RanWorld Voice Lounges:** Spatial audio stage with live speaker spotlight, real-time listener reactions, and room chat.
5. **Trust Engine & Creator Economy:** Transparent trust scores, 18+ age verification protection gate, and monetized creator rewards.
